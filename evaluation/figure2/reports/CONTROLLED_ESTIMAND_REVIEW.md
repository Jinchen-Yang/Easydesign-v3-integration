# Controlled comparison: what can change the outcome?

If all methods receive identical target/site/native generator conditions and draw the same
candidate distribution from the same backend, a harness cannot systematically improve
accepted/all-generated yield merely by re-ranking those same candidates. It can affect
workflow correctness, failure handling and whether a campaign is completed. This is the
estimand of the initial, 280-candidate-per-method queue.

The scientific interpretation loop requires a separate prospective estimand with a real
decision opportunity before additional generation. A suitable next protocol is two waves:
280 first-pilot candidates (7 × 40 × 1), followed by 280 equally budgeted designs for every
method. Keep target/site/context/evaluator fixed. Let Full and Plain choose among the same
predeclared supported strategy interventions after seeing only their first-wave outputs;
fixed pipeline follows its predeclared deterministic rule. Count both waves in the campaign
denominator and report both final-wave and whole-campaign yield. No method gets extra retries
or a larger hidden generation budget. Hold independent evaluator outputs out of the agent's
adaptation loop to prevent endpoint leakage.

This extension doubles the queue to 65,520 planned generated designs and must have its own
versioned protocol, fixed action space, prompts, total budget and cost preflight before any
generation. It is not currently authorized or included in the inert initial-wave queue.
The present benchmark does not assert that the 280-candidate test answers the loop-quality
question. End-to-end site/strategy choices remain a separate estimand.
